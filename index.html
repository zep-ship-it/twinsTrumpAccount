Their story

Meet Abigail and Gabrielle, born in 2017. Abigail and Gabrielle are nine and already dreaming big: Abigail wants to be a Math Teacher, Gabrielle wants to be a Fashion Designer. We can't wait to see where they go, and we'd love your help giving them a little something to grow alongside their dreams.

We're inviting the people who love them to start something that will still be growing when they're grown. Every gift, big or small, is a vote of confidence in their future.

What your gift does

Each girl has her own Trump Account, a federal savings and investment account for children under 18. Gifts are invested and stay put until adulthood, so they have years to grow. Think of it as a present they'll open at 18. This page isn't affiliated with the program or the government. It's a family fundraiser.

How to give

  1. Choose which girl you'd like to give to, or give to both.

One thing to know

Each girl can receive up to $5,000 per year from all donors combined. If you're planning a larger gift, please check with Tiffany (mother) first so we stay within the limit. Gifts are personal gifts and aren't tax-deductible.

Thank you

Questions

What is a Trump Account?

A federal savings and investment account for a child under 18. Funds stay in the account until adulthood, and each child has their own account.

Can I give to just one girl?

Yes. Use the button for the girl you'd like to give to.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

No. It's a personal gift, not a charitable donation.
